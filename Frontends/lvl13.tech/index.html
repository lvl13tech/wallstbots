Bot13: The Proprietary Daily Trading Bot

Bot13 is our flagship algorithmic trading system. It monitors market conditions every single day across stocks, AI & quantum equities, and cryptocurrency — identifying statistical edges and acting on them with precision.

What makes Bot13 different isn't just when it trades — it's when it doesn't.

The Edge Rule: Bot13 does not trade on days without a confirmed statistical edge. Instead of forcing positions into uncertain conditions, it stays flat — preserving capital and waiting for high-confidence setups. This is how it avoids the unnecessary losses that most bots can't avoid.
